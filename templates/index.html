What I do

Actor systems in Rust, from design to production.

I review the design of actor-based and event-sourced systems. Who supervises whom, what a restart loses, how state is recovered and what happens to messages in flight are the questions I start with.

I write code with your team, in your codebase. That can be a new service, a framework choice or a migration of an existing system.

I also take on general backend and distributed-systems work in Rust.
