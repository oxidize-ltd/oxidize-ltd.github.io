## Controller

Oxidize Limited, Gladstonos 16, 8046 Paphos, Cyprus, is the data controller for any personal data
you send us. Contact: [info@oxidize.cy](mailto:info@oxidize.cy).

## What we process and why

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and the contents of your message in order
to reply to your inquiry and, where relevant, to perform a contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and
(f)). We keep correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose and applicable record-keeping
obligations.

## Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking on this site. Fonts are served from this
site. If you choose a color theme, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage and never
leaves your device.

## Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data, or object to its processing, by
writing to [info@oxidize.cy](mailto:info@oxidize.cy). You also have the right to lodge a complaint
with the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Cyprus.
